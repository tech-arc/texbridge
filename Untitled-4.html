Our Mission

Bridging the Supply Gap

India currently faces a massive supply gap of 3.77 million tonnes in textile waste. While the traditional linear model of collection remains stagnant and inefficient, TexBridge introduces a revolutionary circular approach.

By formalizing the 60-70% of waste currently handled by the informal sector, our organized network is projected to exponentially increase recovery rates. We are not just collecting waste; we are building a robust infrastructure that turns discarded fabric into a valuable resource for the future.

Our Process

Preserving Jobs, Improving Efficiency

We don't replace the informal sector; we empower it. By connecting waste pickers (Kabadiwalas) to Dry Waste Collection Centers (DWCCs) and Textile Recovery Facilities (TRFs), we create a seamless supply chain.

1. Efficient Collection

Our app directs verified collectors to households based on data. Waste is retrieved and sent to local Dry Waste Collection Centers (DWCC).

2. Smart Segregation

At TRFs, waste is categorized: 50% for Downcycling, 20% for Recycling, and 15% Rewearable. We ensure zero landfill contribution.

3. Industry Connection

TRFs connect directly with buyers. Manufacturers bid on sorted waste (Cotton, Denim, Nylon) for new production cycles.
