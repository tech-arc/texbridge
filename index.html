Our Mission

Bridging the Supply Gap

India currently faces a massive supply gap of 3.77 million tonnes in textile waste. While the traditional linear model of collection remains stagnant and inefficient, RE-TEX introduces a revolutionary circular approach.

By formalizing the 60-70% of waste currently handled by the informal sector, our organized network is projected to exponentially increase recovery rates. We are not just collecting waste, we are building a robust infrastructure that turns discarded fabric into a valuable resource for the future.

Our Process

Preserving Jobs, Improving Efficiency

We don't replace the informal sector, we empower it. By connecting waste pickers to Dry Waste Collection Centers (DWCCs) and Textile Recovery Facilities (TRFs), we create a seamless supply chain.

Efficient Collection

Verified collectors pick up waste from homes & send it to local hubs.

Smart Segregation

Waste sorted: Downcycling 30%, Recycling 20%, Upcycling 30%, Rewearable 15%, Residual 5%. Zero landfill.

Industry Connection

Sorted materials reach manufacturers who bid for new production cycles.

Custom Upcycling

Recycled fibers transformed into trendy, ready-to-wear apparel.

Transparent Tracking

Users track donations & recycling through the Retex app.

Community Impact

Supports artisans, NGOs, and local jobs while promoting sustainable fashion.
